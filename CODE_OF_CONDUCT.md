# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for all participants in the **Buckshot Tracker** project, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Standards

Participants in the project are expected to:
- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility, apologize to those affected by mistakes, and learn from the experience.
- Focus on what is best not just for any individual, but for the overall community.

Unacceptable behaviors include harassment, offensive comments, personal attacks, publishing others' private information without explicit permission, and disruptive or unprofessional conduct.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the repository maintainers via GitHub issues or project communication channels. All reports will be reviewed promptly and treated with discretion.
